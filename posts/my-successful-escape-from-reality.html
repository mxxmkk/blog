My successful escape from reality

Once I got to my hostel I swiftly conquered my social anxiety and I met so many people, I saw such awesome places, I spent my time like never before. Writing those lines right now, sitting in a café and waiting for the train to take me back to my regular life, I cannot stop myself from thinking, that I made a big mistake by coming here. How can I possibly leave this life, those people, this drugging feeling of freedom.


I fell in love in Prague - I am sure this feeling will not last for long and I will probably never meet this person ever again even though both of us got each other's contact info. Letting people go was never this hard. I wish I didn't care. I wish that was all a distant dream, that I was never meant to reach. And here lies the issue: it is not very distant and it is sure thing not unreal, quite the opposite, leaving my old life behind sounds so much easier than staying.


The initial idea behind this trip was to restart myself and once I got back start imposing my life by acting, by doing anything with my life and free time - well mission has been successfully failed and I don't want to go back. I wish everyone who reads or will never read that, will find that something for themselves and it will change you as it did to me.
