Max's journal
new and old

If someone asks me about my favourite author or a book series I will honestly answer "The Chronicles of Amber" by Roger Zelazny or "The Invisible Man" by H. G. Wells. But those novels were recommended to me while I was a kid. Now, I have a bittersweet feeling that I will never find a novel or a story that will truly affect me in the same way. I do have books that are found great in the recent years but I feel that somehow they will never come close to "my classics".


What about modern writers and novels? I am going to be honest - I don't know how do people find the "hidden gems" or new books from new authors to read. I feel like it was easier back then. In a way I am stuck in the past reading relatively old books without any exposure to new talents and the new style of writing.


Right now I am planning on re-reading "The Chronicles of Amber" to see if they are as good as I remember. Somehow I doubt that our generation is capable of producing sth grand and deep like "The Doll", Boleslaw Prus and "The Trial", Franz Kafka. Maybe I am just romanticising the past but anyway, I do hope that when the time comes I will have the courage and content to write my own book and have my own physical copy on my shelf.
