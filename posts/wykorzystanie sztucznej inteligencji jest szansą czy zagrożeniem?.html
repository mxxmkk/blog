Usage of AI - an opportunity or a threat?

My version Rapid development in machine learning has brought large language models (hereafter referred as LLMs) with the fascinating capability of producing human-like texts. Furthermore they have become to be able to do other tasks like: answering emails, writing code, generating images. Thus, people, have been divided: Those who see an opportunity and those who are afraid of displacing workers. Personally, in the current state I do not believe that AI will cause major harm to people by replacing them, posing existential risks or killing art.


First of all, current AI systems do not possess autonomous reasoning or consciousness and therefore should be viewed primarily as tools rather than independent agents. They are complex computation models that predict the most likely continuation of a text based on patterns found in training data. Their capabilities are ultimately limited by the instructions they receive and by the expertise of the person using them. Hence, it is not AI itself that poses the greatest threat, but rather the people in charge of developing and deploying it. In terms of safety, malicious actors may use these systems to spread misinformation, automate cyberattacks, or create increasingly convincing scams. However, a model is not a threat in a vacuum - it is merely a tool whose impact depends on the intentions of it’s operator.


Secondly, as a smart-tool LLMs are a magnificent productivity enchantmcment for coders and managers that can outsource the dull but time consuming corporate tasks. As generative models will become better they will lower the cost of production by making a single worker - more efficient.


Next - artistic and cultural aspect. Generative models learned to mimic artists and their craft but the assets created all have near identical characteristics like: saturated colors, doll-like people with plastic skin with additional fingers, uncanny valley effect - in the last years the demand for authentic hand crafted items has been only rising.


In conclusion, despite the concerns surrounding artificial intelligence I believe that its benefits outweigh its risks. Whether in the fields of safety, employment, or culture, AI remains a tool rather than an independent actor. Consequently, the future impact of this technology will depend not on the models themselves, but on the people who choose how to use them.


My new version Rapid development in machine learning has brought large language models (hereafter referred as LLMs) with the fascinating capability of producing human-like texts. Furthermore they have become to be able to do other tasks like: answering emails, writing code, generating images. While this has sparked widespread anxiety regarding automation and the future of work, I view this technological shift as an extraordinary breakthrough. Rather than replacing humanity, AI serves as an invaluable partner that will drive efficiency, fortify security, and enrich our cultural landscape.


First of all, in terms of security, AI should be recognized as a potential defensive asset rather than an autonomous threat. In the hands of cybersecurity experts, it becomes a matchless shield. Machine learning algorithms can analyze network traffic, predict vulnerabilities, and neutralize cyberattacks with a speed and precision that human operators simply cannot match. Therefore, rather than endangering our digital safety, AI acts as a tireless guard, significantly strengthening our defenses.


Secondly, as a sophisticated tool, LLMs offer a magnificent productivity enhancement for professionals such as coders and managers. By outsourcing dull but time-consuming corporate tasks to AI, workers can heavily optimize their daily routines. As generative models continue to improve, they will drastically lower the cost of production by making a single worker far more efficient, ultimately revolutionizing the modern workplace for the better.


Next - artistic and cultural aspect. Generative models learned to mimic artists and their craft but rather than replacing traditional artists, generative models serve as a boundless source of inspiration, allowing creators to explore novel concepts and styles rapidly. While early models struggled with the "uncanny valley" effect, today's AI democratises creativity, enabling anyone to bring their imagination to life.


In conclusion, artificial intelligence is an incredibly powerful tool with immense potential that will only expand its capabilities and unfold over the years. As we continue to develop these systems, their role in enhancing our daily lives will become increasingly vital
